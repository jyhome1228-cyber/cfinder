## Included weights

- `A2Z-Bold.woff2`: original `에이투지체-7Bold.woff2`; OS/2 weight 700; internal family `A2Z 7 Bold`.
- `A2Z-Black.woff2`: original `에이투지체-9Black.woff2`; OS/2 weight 900; internal family `A2Z 9 Black`.

Use these as two weights of a CSS `A2Z` family with `font-display: swap`. Files contain 11,676 glyphs each.
