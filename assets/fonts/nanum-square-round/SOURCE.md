- Official stylesheet linked by the catalog: https://hangeul.naver.com/css/fonts.css?5
- `NanumSquareRound-Regular.woff2`: https://hangeul.pstatic.net/hangeul_static/webfont/NanumSquareRound/NanumSquareRoundR.woff2
- `NanumSquareRound-Bold.woff2`: https://hangeul.pstatic.net/hangeul_static/webfont/NanumSquareRound/NanumSquareRoundB.woff2

## Included weights

- Regular: OS/2 weight 400; internal family `NanumSquareRound Regular`.
- Bold: OS/2 weight 700; internal family `NanumSquareRound Bold`.

Use these as two weights of a CSS `NanumSquareRound` family with `font-display: swap`. Files contain 15,545 glyphs each.
